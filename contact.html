Before you visit

Short answers

Do you coach commerce or humanities?

Not this year. The timetable is foundation, Class 10, Class 12 science (PCM and PCB), NEET, JEE, and the pre-board crash course.

Can we pay the fee in parts?

Yes. Three instalments. The first is due before the first class. Dates are written on the admission note, not left as a verbal promise.

Which language is the class in?

English. Teachers switch to Tamil when a student is stuck. State Board students who take the Tamil paper are taught that subject in Tamil.

Is there a free demo class?

One trial class, after a twenty-minute counselling conversation. We do not take drop-ins in the middle of someone else’s lesson.

What should we bring?

The latest school test, the school timetable, and a parent or guardian. Repeaters can bring their last NEET or JEE scorecard if they have one.

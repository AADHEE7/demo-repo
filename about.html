About the centre

Started with one physics batch. Still on the same floor.

Aadhav Academy is an independent coaching centre in T. Nagar. The teachers you meet in June are the teachers who mark the last paper. There is no rotating franchise staff.

The story

A room where a quiet student is a signal, not a relief.

Meenakshi Subramanian opened the centre in 2014 after eight years teaching physics at a junior college in Mylapore. She wanted batches small enough that someone notices who is stuck on the previous chapter.

The centre occupies the first floor of a quiet building off Venkatanarayana Road, a short walk from the T. Nagar bus terminus. Three classrooms, one hall for Saturday tests, and a lobby where phones stay in a numbered pouch.

Years

How the timetable grew.

  1. 2014One Class 12 physics batch, sixteen students, rented for three hours a day.
  2. 2017Maths and chemistry joined. The centre moved to the floor it still uses.
  3. 2019Class 10 board sections opened for both CBSE and State Board.
  4. 2021A NEET biology desk started, taught from the NCERT text.
  5. 2026About 180 students on the roll, and a pre-board crash course from November.

What we will not do

The limits are part of the teaching.

  • No batch above 28, even in March
  • No commerce, accountancy, or humanities course this year
  • No promise of a rank, a college, or a scholarship
  • No class during a student’s school hours
  • No recording of the lesson for sale

A teaching day

Evening batch, from the terminus to the last doubt.

4:15 pm, lobby

Students sign the register and leave phones in a pouch. Drinking water is on the landing. The rooms are air-conditioned; the lobby is not.

4:30 pm, concept

One idea, written on the board, then five questions that use it. Latecomers after 4:40 wait for the next meeting rather than walking in mid-explanation.

7:15 pm, doubt table

The teacher stays thirty minutes. Questions that need a full chapter go into Saturday’s clinic, so the next batch can start on time.

Come and see the floor

Counselling is twenty minutes, with the latest answer paper on the table.
